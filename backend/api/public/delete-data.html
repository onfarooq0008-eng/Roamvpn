Data & Deletion

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Roam VPN has no accounts, so there is no profile to delete. This page explains what exists and how to remove it.

What exists on our side

  • While you are connected: your device's WireGuard public key, an assigned private tunnel address and a registration token.
  • Standard technical server logs, described in the Privacy Policy.

How it is removed

  • Disconnect in the app. The app asks our server to remove your registration straight away.
  • Automatic clean-up. Unfinished registrations are removed within about an hour, and any registration not used for 30 days is removed automatically.
  • On your phone. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to delete your private key, settings and favourites. A new key is created if you install again.
  • Ad data. Reset your advertising ID in Android settings. Data held by Yandex is handled under its own policy.

Ask us to delete or show data

Email admin@fastvpnn.pp.ua with the subject "Data request". Because we do not know who you are, tell us the app version and the approximate date and time you connected so we can look for a matching registration or log entry. We will reply to say what we found and what we removed.

We cannot delete what we do not store: browsing activity through the VPN is not recorded.
