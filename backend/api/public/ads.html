Ads & Third Parties

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Why there are ads

Roam VPN is free. Ads pay for the servers and development, so there is no subscription and no account.

What you will see

  • a sponsored card in the server list on the Home screen and one in Locations;
  • a full-screen ad after every third time you disconnect (never while you are connecting or switching servers).

Who provides them

All ads are served by Yandex Mobile Ads (Yandex Advertising Network). Yandex may use your device's advertising ID and technical data (such as IP address, device model and OS version, and ad interactions) to choose and measure ads. Its handling of data is described in Yandex's Privacy Policy. We do not send Yandex your browsing activity, and the app does not read it.

When ads start

No ad SDK is started and no ad is requested until you tap "Agree & Continue" on the first screen of the app.

Your controls

  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalization in Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads (the wording varies by phone).
  • Report an ad you think is inappropriate or misleading: email admin@fastvpnn.pp.ua with a screenshot if you can.
  • Uninstalling the app stops all ads.

Other third-party components

The app uses the open-source WireGuard tunnel library and standard Android libraries. These run on your device and do not send us extra data.
